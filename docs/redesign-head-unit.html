The Way · redesign proposal · 1 Nov

Make it read like
a head unit

I went through all fifteen pages at phone width. The bones are good: black ground, square edges, green, amber and red that mean something. What drags is sentences where a number should be, status painted across whole cards, and buttons that wrap into odd stacks. The direction: lay every page out the way a bike computer lays out data fields. One label, one big number, one colour that tells you whether to worry.

Now = the live site tonightProposed = the mockup beside itNumbers are yours from 1 Nov
Rule 1

Numbers in a condensed face

A narrow display face, the kind bike computers use, so 205 / 187 fits big in a third of the screen. Body text stays a plain sans.

205 / 187 g
Rule 2

Status is a stripe, not a fill

A 3 px edge and a coloured number say amber or red. The card behind stays black, so a red day doesn't turn the whole page red.

9:45aZone 1, 150 min83 of 150 min55%
Rule 3

One sentence, then a fold

Each card gets one line that says what to do. The reasoning sits under "why", closed until you ask for it.

Drink 500 ml before 9p. + why
Rule 4

Fixed status column

Every row has the same right-hand column for its chip, so your eye runs straight down and the titles get the full width.

Rule 5

Logging is a grid of tiles

Same-size tiles, two or four across, each with a picture and the amount. No more buttons wrapping into three ragged rows.

Rule 6

Targets are ticks

Every bar has a white tick at its target. Past the tick it keeps going, hatched, so "over" is visible without reading.

How I'd ship it

  1. Now, as a fix: the Settings overflow (the page is 443 px wide on a 390 px phone).
  2. Step 1, the system (one push): the number face, stripes instead of filled cards, the fixed status column, target ticks. Every page picks these up at once. No new data, no new logic.
  3. Step 2, page by page, one a day: Day, then Food, Hydration, Fuel, Ride, Calendar, Lift, the body pages, Settings. Each gets checked at 390 px and screenshotted before the next.
  4. Freeze note: this is formatting, not features. The lift steppers and per-set ticks are the one new behaviour; I'd hold those for after 15 Nov unless you say go.
